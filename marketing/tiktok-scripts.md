## POSTING STRATEGY

### Best Times to Post (South Africa)
- Morning: 7:00-9:00 AM (commute time)
- Lunch: 12:00-1:00 PM
- Evening: 6:00-8:00 PM (after work)
- Sunday: 7:00-9:00 PM (planning for the week)

### Content Mix
- 40% Educational (tax tips, deductions)
- 30% Entertainment (relatable content, trends)
- 20% Social proof (results, testimonials)
