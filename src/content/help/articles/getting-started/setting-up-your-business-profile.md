# Setting Up Your Business Profile

Configure your company details to ensure accurate tax calculations and SARS-compliant reporting.

## Overview

Your business profile contains essential information that ProcessX uses to:
- Calculate your tax obligations correctly
- Generate compliant reports for SARS
- Customize your dashboard and reports
- Enable VAT calculations if you're VAT registered

## Accessing Business Settings

1. Log in to your ProcessX account
2. Navigate to **Settings** from the dashboard
3. Select **Company Settings** or click on your company name

## Required Information

### Business Details

| Field | Description | Example |
|-------|-------------|---------|
| Business Name | Your registered business name | "ABC Consulting (Pty) Ltd" |
| Trading Name | Name you trade under (if different) | "ABC Consulting" |
| Business Type | Sole proprietor, Company, CC, etc. | "Sole Proprietor" |
| Industry | Your primary business sector | "Professional Services" |

### Tax Information

| Field | Description | Notes |
|-------|-------------|-------|
| Income Tax Number | Your SARS income tax reference | Optional but recommended |
| VAT Number | Your VAT registration number | Required if VAT registered |
| Tax Year End | Your financial year end date | Usually February for individuals |

### Contact Details

- **Physical Address**: Your business address
- **Postal Address**: Where you receive mail
- **Phone Number**: Business contact number
- **Email**: Business email address

## VAT Registration

If you're VAT registered (turnover exceeds R1 million annually or you've voluntarily registered):

1. Toggle **VAT Registered** to Yes
2. Enter your VAT registration number
3. Select your VAT filing period (monthly or bi-monthly)
4. ProcessX will automatically calculate VAT on income and expenses

## Multiple Companies

With the Business or Enterprise plan, you can manage multiple companies:

1. Click **Add Company** in the company switcher
2. Fill in the new company's details
3. Switch between companies using the dropdown in the navigation

## Best Practices

- **Keep information current**: Update your profile whenever business details change
- **Verify tax numbers**: Double-check your tax and VAT numbers for accuracy
- **Complete all fields**: More complete profiles generate more accurate reports
