# Creating Your Account

Get started with ProcessX by creating your free account in just a few minutes.

## Overview

Creating a ProcessX account gives you access to powerful expense tracking, income management, and tax compliance tools designed specifically for South African businesses and freelancers.

## Step-by-Step Guide

### 1. Visit the Sign-Up Page

Navigate to [processx.co.za](https://processx.co.za) and click the **Get Started** or **Sign Up** button in the top navigation.

### 2. Enter Your Details

Fill in the registration form with:
- **Full Name**: Your legal name as it appears on official documents
- **Email Address**: Use a valid email you have access to
- **Password**: Create a strong password with at least 8 characters

### 3. Verify Your Email

After submitting the form:
1. Check your email inbox for a verification email from ProcessX
2. Click the verification link in the email
3. Your account will be activated automatically

> **Tip**: If you don't see the verification email, check your spam or junk folder.

### 4. Complete Your Profile

Once verified, you'll be prompted to:
- Add your business name
- Select your business type
- Enter your tax registration number (optional at this stage)

## What's Included in Your Free Account

With the Starter plan, you get:
- Unlimited expense tracking
- Basic income recording
- Dashboard with financial overview
- Mobile-friendly access
- SARS-compliant exports
