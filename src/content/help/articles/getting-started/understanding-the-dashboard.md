# Understanding the Dashboard

Navigate your ProcessX dashboard and make the most of your financial overview.

## Overview

The ProcessX dashboard is your command center for managing business finances. It provides a real-time snapshot of your income, expenses, and overall financial health.

## Dashboard Layout

### Top Navigation

- **Company Switcher**: Switch between companies (if you manage multiple)
- **Quick Actions**: Fast access to add expenses or income
- **User Menu**: Account settings, subscription, and logout

### Main Dashboard Sections

## Key Metrics Cards

At the top of your dashboard, you'll see summary cards showing:

| Metric | What It Shows |
|--------|---------------|
| **Total Income** | All income recorded for the selected period |
| **Total Expenses** | All expenses recorded for the selected period |
| **Net Profit/Loss** | Income minus expenses |
| **Estimated Tax** | Your approximate tax liability |

## Time Period Selector

Change the date range to view different periods:
- **This Month**: Current month's data
- **Last Month**: Previous month
- **This Quarter**: Current quarter
- **This Year**: Full tax year
- **Custom**: Select specific date range

## Charts and Visualizations

### Income vs Expenses Chart

A visual comparison of your income and expenses over time, helping you identify:
- Seasonal patterns
- Growth trends
- Problem areas

### Expense Breakdown

A pie or bar chart showing your expenses by category, useful for:
- Identifying largest cost centers
- Finding areas to reduce spending
- Understanding spending patterns

### Income Breakdown

Similar visualization for income sources:
- Which services generate most revenue
- Client concentration
- Revenue diversification

## Recent Activity

A list of your most recent transactions:
- Latest expenses added
- Recent income recorded
- Quick access to edit or view details

## Quick Actions

Common tasks available directly from the dashboard:
- **Add Expense**: Record a new expense
- **Add Income**: Record new revenue
- **View Reports**: Jump to reports section
- **Upload Receipt**: Quick receipt capture

## Dashboard Widgets

Depending on your subscription, you may see additional widgets:

### Vehicle Logbook Summary
- Business vs personal travel ratio
- Total kilometres this period
- Estimated deduction value

### Tax Overview
- Year-to-date taxable income
- Deductions claimed
- Estimated tax payable

### Document Queue
- Documents awaiting analysis
- Pending approvals
- Recent uploads

## Customizing Your View

### Compact vs Expanded View

Some cards can be expanded or collapsed based on your preference.

### Refresh Data

Click the refresh icon to update dashboard data if you've recently added transactions.

## Mobile Dashboard

On mobile devices, the dashboard adapts to:
- Single-column layout
- Swipeable cards
- Collapsible sections
- Touch-friendly navigation
