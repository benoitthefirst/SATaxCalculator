# Setting Up Your Vehicle

Add your vehicle details and configure tracking settings for SARS travel allowance claims.

## Overview

If you receive a travel allowance or use your personal vehicle for business, SARS requires you to keep a detailed logbook. ProcessX makes this easy by helping you:
- Register your vehicle
- Track business trips
- Calculate deductions automatically
- Generate SARS-compliant exports

## Why Keep a Vehicle Logbook?

A logbook is required to:
- Claim the business portion of travel allowance
- Support your deduction if audited
- Prove business vs personal usage
- Calculate accurate deductions

## Adding Your Vehicle

### Step 1: Navigate to Vehicle Logbook

From the dashboard, go to **Vehicle Logbook** in the navigation.

### Step 2: Add New Vehicle

Click **Add Vehicle** or **Set Up Vehicle**.

### Step 3: Enter Vehicle Details

| Field | Description | Example |
|-------|-------------|---------|
| Make | Vehicle manufacturer | Toyota |
| Model | Vehicle model | Corolla |
| Year | Year of manufacture | 2022 |
| Registration | Number plate | CA 123-456 |
| Odometer | Current reading (km) | 45,230 |

### Step 4: Configure Settings

| Setting | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| Fixed Cost | Monthly vehicle costs (insurance, finance, etc.) |
| Fuel Type | Petrol or diesel |
| Fuel Consumption | Average km/litre |

### Step 5: Save

Click **Save Vehicle** to complete setup.

## Vehicle Information Needed

### Basic Details
- **Make and Model**: Identifies your vehicle
- **Year**: Affects deemed cost calculations
- **Registration**: For record-keeping

### Financial Details
- **Purchase Price**: Original cost (or market value if owned before business use)
- **Date Acquired**: When you started using for business
- **Financing**: If on finance, monthly payment details

### Running Costs (Optional)
- **Insurance**: Annual premium
- **License Fees**: Annual cost
- **Maintenance Budget**: Estimated annual maintenance

## Starting Odometer

When setting up, enter your **current odometer reading** accurately:
- This is your starting point
- All trip distances calculate from this
- Get it from your dashboard
- Photo proof recommended

## Multiple Vehicles

If you use more than one vehicle for business:

1. Add each vehicle separately
2. Select the vehicle when logging trips
3. Each generates its own logbook
4. Deductions calculate per vehicle

## Vehicle Ownership

### Company Owned

If the company owns the vehicle:
- Different tax treatment applies
- Fringe benefit may arise
- Consult your accountant
- ProcessX handles personal vehicles primarily

### Personal Vehicle

If you own the vehicle personally:
- You claim based on business use
- Keep detailed records
- Logbook method or fixed cost method

## Updating Vehicle Details

If your vehicle details change:

1. Go to **Vehicle Logbook**
2. Click on the vehicle
3. Click **Edit**
4. Update the relevant fields
5. Save changes

Note: Odometer changes create an audit trail.

## Replacing a Vehicle

If you get a new vehicle:

1. Update end date on old vehicle
2. Add the new vehicle
3. Enter opening odometer
4. Continue logging trips to new vehicle

Historical data from the old vehicle is preserved.
