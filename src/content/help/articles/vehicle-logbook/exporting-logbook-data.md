# Exporting Logbook Data

Export your vehicle logbook for SARS submission and accountant review.

## Overview

At tax time, you'll need to provide your logbook data to:
- Support your travel deduction claim
- Share with your accountant
- Keep for your records
- Submit if requested by [SARS](https://www.sars.gov.za/)

ProcessX makes this easy with comprehensive export options.

## Export Options

### 1. Full Logbook Export

Complete record of all trips:
- Every trip with dates
- Odometer readings
- Business purposes
- Distance calculations

Best for: SARS submission, complete records

### 2. Summary Export

Condensed version showing:
- Total kilometres
- Business kilometres
- Business percentage
- Deduction calculation

Best for: Quick reference, [ITR12](https://www.sars.gov.za/types-of-tax/personal-income-tax/) completion

### 3. Deduction Calculation

Focused export showing:
- Vehicle details
- [SARS rates](https://www.sars.gov.za/tax-rates/employers/travel-allowance/) applied
- Step-by-step calculation
- Final deduction amount

Best for: Tax return, accountant review

## How to Export

### Step 1: Navigate to Vehicle Logbook

Go to **Vehicle Logbook** from the navigation.

### Step 2: Select Export

Click **Export** or **Download**.

### Step 3: Choose Options

| Option | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| Tax Year | Select the year you're exporting |
| Vehicle | If multiple, select which one |
| Format | PDF, Excel, or CSV |
| Type | Full logbook, Summary, or Calculation |

### Step 4: Download

Click **Export** to download your file.

### PDF Format

Best for:

- Make, model, year
- Registration number
- Opening odometer (start of year)
- Closing odometer (end of year)
- Total kilometres

## SARS Requirements

Your logbook export should show (per [SARS requirements](https://www.sars.gov.za/types-of-tax/personal-income-tax/learn-about-your-deductions/)):

### For Each Trip
- Date of travel
- Starting point
- Destination
- Distance travelled
- Business purpose

### Annual Summary
- Total kilometres for year
- Total business kilometres
- Business use percentage

### Vehicle Details
- Description of vehicle
- Date business use commenced

## Tips for Clean Exports

1. **Complete all trips**: Fill in any missing details before export
2. **Verify totals**: Check odometer readings make sense
3. **Review purposes**: Ensure business purposes are clear
4. **Check dates**: All trips should be within tax year
5. **Update vehicle info**: Confirm details are correct

## Sharing with Your Accountant

When sending to your accountant:

1. Export PDF for easy reading
2. Export Excel if they need data
3. Include both Full Logbook and Summary
4. Add the Deduction Calculation
5. Use secure file sharing

Need a registered tax practitioner? Find one on the [SARS website](https://www.sars.gov.za/find-a-tax-practitioner/).

## Keeping Records

[SARS requires](https://www.sars.gov.za/businesses-and-employers/my-business-and-tax/record-keeping/) you to keep records for 5 years:
- Download and store exports
- Keep backup copies
- Organize by tax year
- Include supporting documents
