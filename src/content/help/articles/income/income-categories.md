# Income Categories

Classify income by source and type for better reporting and tax compliance.

## Overview

Categorizing your income helps you:
- Understand where your money comes from
- Generate meaningful reports
- Identify your most profitable services
- Complete your tax return accurately

## Default Income Categories

ProcessX includes pre-configured categories to cover most business income types:

### Service-Based Income

| Category | Description | Examples |
|----------|-------------|----------|
| Professional Services | Fees for professional work | Consulting, legal, accounting |
| Freelance Work | Contract or gig work | Design, writing, development |
| Commission | Sales or referral commissions | Agent fees, affiliate income |

### Product-Based Income

| Category | Description | Examples |
|----------|-------------|----------|
| Product Sales | Physical goods sold | Retail, wholesale, handmade |
| Digital Products | Digital goods sold | eBooks, courses, software |

### Passive Income

| Category | Description | Examples |
|----------|-------------|----------|
| Rental Income | Property or equipment rental | Office space, equipment hire |
| Royalties | Licensing and usage fees | Patents, content licensing |
| Interest | Interest earned | Business savings, investments |
| Dividends | Share dividends | Investment returns |

### Other Income

| Category | Description | Examples |
|----------|-------------|----------|
| Grants | Business grants received | Government grants, awards |
| Other Income | Miscellaneous business income | Refunds, insurance payouts |

## Choosing Categories

When recording income, select the category that best describes the **source** of that income:

### Example Scenarios

| Received | Category |
|----------|----------|
| Payment for web design project | Professional Services |
| Sale of office equipment | Other Income |
| Monthly rental from subletting | Rental Income |
| Referral fee from partner | Commission |
| Interest from business account | Interest |

## Category Analytics

View your income breakdown by category:

1. Go to **Income** > **Analytics**
2. Select the time period
3. View the category breakdown chart
4. See percentages and totals per category

This helps you understand:
- Which services are most profitable
- Income diversification
- Trends over time

## SARS Tax Treatment

Different income types may have different tax treatments:

| Category | Tax Treatment |
|----------|--------------|
| Professional Services | Fully taxable as income |
| Product Sales | Fully taxable as income |
| Interest | Taxable with exemption (first R23,800 for under-65s) |
| Dividends | Subject to Dividends Tax (usually withheld) |
| Rental Income | Fully taxable as income |
| Capital Gains | Separate calculation (excluded from ProcessX income) |

## Using Categories for Reports

### Income Statement
Categories appear in your profit & loss report, showing revenue breakdown.

### Tax Computation
Categories help allocate income correctly for tax calculations.

### Client Reports
Combine category and client data to see which clients pay for which services.

## Best Practices

1. **Be consistent**: Use the same category for similar income
2. **Don't over-categorize**: Stick to standard categories
3. **Use descriptions**: Add details in the description field
4. **Review quarterly**: Ensure categories are being used correctly
