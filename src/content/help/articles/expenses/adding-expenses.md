# Adding Expenses

Learn the different ways to add and categorize business expenses in ProcessX.

## Overview

ProcessX offers multiple ways to record expenses, making it easy to keep your records up to date no matter how you prefer to work.

## Methods for Adding Expenses

### 1. Manual Entry

The most common method for adding expenses:

1. Go to **Expenses** from the navigation
2. Click **Add Expense**
3. Fill in the expense details
4. Click **Save**

### 2. Receipt Upload with AI Analysis

Let ProcessX extract details from your receipts:

1. Go to **Documents** > **Analyze**
2. Upload a receipt image or PDF
3. ProcessX extracts vendor, amount, date, and category
4. Review and approve the suggested expense
5. The expense is automatically created

### 3. Quick Add from Dashboard

For fast entry:

1. Click the **+** or **Quick Add** button on your dashboard
2. Enter basic details (amount, description, category)
3. Save immediately or add more details later

### 4. Recurring Expenses

For regular monthly expenses:

1. Set up a recurring expense once
2. ProcessX automatically creates the expense each period
3. Review and approve or let it auto-approve

## Expense Form Fields

| Field | Required | Description |
|-------|----------|-------------|
| Description | Yes | What you purchased |
| Amount | Yes | Total cost including VAT |
| Date | Yes | Purchase date |
| Category | Yes | Type of expense |
| Vendor | No | Store or supplier name |
| Receipt | No | Proof of purchase |
| Notes | No | Additional details |

## Choosing the Right Category

Selecting the correct category ensures accurate tax calculations:

### Common Categories

- **Office Supplies**: Stationery, ink, paper
- **Travel & Accommodation**: Flights, hotels, transport
- **Meals & Entertainment**: Client meals, team events
- **Marketing & Advertising**: Ads, promotions, signage
- **Professional Services**: Accountant, legal, consulting fees
- **Software & Subscriptions**: Apps, tools, services
- **Telephone & Internet**: Phone, data, connectivity
- **Utilities**: Electricity, water (home office portion)

## Handling VAT on Expenses

If your business is VAT registered:

1. Enter the **VAT-inclusive** amount
2. ProcessX calculates the VAT portion automatically
3. VAT input is tracked for your VAT return

For non-VAT registered businesses:
- Simply enter the full amount paid
- No VAT calculations are applied

## Expense Attachments

Attach supporting documents to any expense:

- **Receipts**: Tax invoices, till slips
- **Invoices**: Supplier invoices
- **Statements**: Monthly service statements
- **Other**: Any relevant documentation

Supported formats: JPG, PNG, PDF (up to 10MB)

## Editing Expenses

To modify an existing expense:

1. Go to **Expenses**
2. Click on the expense to view details
3. Click **Edit**
4. Make your changes
5. Click **Save**

## Deleting Expenses

To remove an incorrect expense:

1. Open the expense
2. Click **Delete**
3. Confirm the deletion

> **Note**: Deleted expenses cannot be recovered. Consider editing instead if you need to correct details.

## Best Practices

1. **Add expenses immediately**: Record while details are fresh
2. **Always attach receipts**: Required for SARS compliance
3. **Use accurate descriptions**: Helps with searching and reporting
4. **Review regularly**: Check for missing or duplicate entries
5. **Categorize correctly**: Affects your tax deductions
