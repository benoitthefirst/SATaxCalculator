# Exporting Expenses for SARS

Generate SARS-compliant expense reports and exports for your tax return.

## Overview

When it's time to file your tax return or meet with your accountant, ProcessX makes it easy to export your expense data in formats that meet [SARS (South African Revenue Service)](https://www.sars.gov.za/) requirements.

## Export Options

### 1. CSV Export

Ideal for:
- Sharing with accountants
- Importing into other software
- Detailed analysis in Excel

### 2. PDF Report

Ideal for:
- Printing for records
- Submitting as supporting documents
- Professional presentation

### 3. Summary Report

Ideal for:
- Quick overview for tax return
- Category totals for [ITR12](https://www.sars.gov.za/types-of-tax/personal-income-tax/)
- Year-end review

## How to Export Expenses

### Step 1: Go to Reports or Expenses

You can export from two locations:
- **Reports** > **Expense Summary**
- **Expenses** > **Export** button

### Step 2: Select Date Range

Choose the tax year you're exporting for:
- **2024/2025 Tax Year**: March 2024 - February 2025
- **Custom Range**: Select specific dates

Learn more about [tax year dates on SARS](https://www.sars.gov.za/types-of-tax/personal-income-tax/tax-season/).

### Step 3: Apply Filters (Optional)

Narrow down your export:
- By category
- By vendor
- By amount range
- Include/exclude specific items

### Step 4: Choose Format

Select your preferred format:
- **CSV**: Spreadsheet format
- **PDF**: Document format
- **Excel**: .xlsx file

### Step 5: Download

Click **Export** or **Download** to get your file.

## Understanding the Export

### CSV/Excel Fields

| Column | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| Date | Expense date |
| Description | What was purchased |
| Category | Expense category |
| Vendor | Where purchased |
| Amount | Total including VAT |
| VAT | VAT portion (if applicable) |
| Net Amount | Amount excluding VAT |

### PDF Report Sections

1. **Summary**: Total expenses, count, categories
2. **Category Breakdown**: Totals by category
3. **Detailed List**: All expenses with details
4. **Receipt Reference**: Links to uploaded receipts

## SARS ITR12 Categories

When completing your [ITR12 tax return](https://www.sars.gov.za/types-of-tax/personal-income-tax/), you'll need totals for specific categories. ProcessX maps to SARS codes:

| ProcessX Category | SARS ITR12 Section |
|-------------------|-------------------|
| Office Supplies | Paragraph (a) - Other expenses |
| Professional Services | Paragraph (a) - Legal/Accounting |
| Travel | Paragraph (a) - Travel expenses |
| Vehicle (actual) | Paragraph (a) - Motor vehicle expenses |
| Vehicle (logbook) | Travel Allowance section |
| Home Office | Paragraph (a) - Home office expenses |

For detailed guidance on allowable deductions, see the [SARS guide on business expenses](https://www.sars.gov.za/types-of-tax/personal-income-tax/learn-about-your-deductions/).

## Deduction Summary Report

For easy tax filing, use the Deduction Summary:

1. Go to **Reports** > **Deduction Summary**
2. Select the tax year
3. View totals already grouped for your ITR12
4. Export the summary

This report shows:
- Total deductible expenses
- Non-deductible items (excluded)
- Category totals matching SARS requirements
- Supporting notes

## What to Give Your Accountant

When sharing with your accountant, provide:

1. **Expense Summary (PDF)**: Overview and totals
2. **Detailed Export (CSV/Excel)**: Full transaction list
3. **Receipts**: Access to uploaded receipts or export
4. **Income Summary**: If they need the full picture

Need a registered tax practitioner? Find one on the [SARS website](https://www.sars.gov.za/find-a-tax-practitioner/).

## Keeping Records

SARS requires you to keep records for **5 years**. See the [SARS record-keeping requirements](https://www.sars.gov.za/businesses-and-employers/my-business-and-tax/record-keeping/). ProcessX stores:
- All expense data
- Uploaded receipts
- Export history
- Report archives

You can always re-export historical data.

## Provisional Tax Payments

For [provisional tax (IRP6)](https://www.sars.gov.za/types-of-tax/provisional-tax/), export quarterly:
- First period: March - August
- Second period: September - February
- Third period (top-up): After year-end
