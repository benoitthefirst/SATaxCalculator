# Updating Your Profile

Edit your personal details and account information in ProcessX.

## Overview

Your profile contains your personal information used for:
- Account identification
- Communication preferences
- Security settings
- Contact information

## Accessing Profile Settings

1. Click your **name** or **avatar** in the top navigation
2. Select **Settings** or **Profile**
3. Or navigate to **Settings** > **Profile**

## Profile Information

### Personal Details

| Field | Description | Editable |
|-------|-------------|----------|
| Full Name | Your legal name | Yes |
| Email Address | Primary contact email | Yes* |
| Phone Number | Contact number | Yes |
| Profile Photo | Avatar image | Yes |

*Email changes may require verification

### Display Preferences

| Setting | Options |
|---------|---------|
| Display Name | How your name appears |
| Timezone | For timestamps |
| Date Format | DD/MM/YYYY or MM/DD/YYYY |
| Currency Display | ZAR formatting |

## Updating Your Name

1. Go to **Settings** > **Profile**
2. Click **Edit** next to your name
3. Enter your updated name
4. Click **Save**

Your name appears on:
- Dashboard welcome
- Team member lists
- Reports (if enabled)
- Account communications

## Updating Your Email

Changing your email address:

1. Go to **Settings** > **Profile**
2. Click **Change Email**
3. Enter your new email address
4. Confirm your current password
5. Check your new email for verification
6. Click the verification link

Until verified, your old email remains active.

## Updating Phone Number

1. Go to **Settings** > **Profile**
2. Click **Edit** next to phone number
3. Enter your new number
4. Click **Save**

Phone number is used for:
- Account recovery
- Important notifications (if enabled)
- Two-factor authentication (if enabled)

## Profile Photo

Add a profile photo:

1. Click on your avatar or photo placeholder
2. Click **Upload Photo**
3. Select an image from your device
4. Crop if needed
5. Click **Save**

Requirements:
- JPG, PNG, or GIF
- Maximum 5MB
- Square ratio recommended

## Notification Preferences

Control how ProcessX contacts you:

| Notification Type | Options |
|-------------------|---------|
| Email notifications | On/Off |
| Product updates | On/Off |
| Tax reminders | On/Off |
| Weekly summaries | On/Off |

Manage at **Settings** > **Notifications**

## Linked Accounts

If you signed up with Google or social login:
- Your profile syncs with that account
- Some details may not be editable
- You can add a password for email login

## Account Deletion

To delete your account:

1. Go to **Settings** > **Profile**
2. Scroll to **Danger Zone**
3. Click **Delete Account**
4. Confirm by typing your email
5. Click **Permanently Delete**

Warning: This action is irreversible. All data will be deleted.

## Data Export Before Deletion

Before deleting, export your data:
1. Go to **Settings** > **Data Export**
2. Click **Export All Data**
3. Download the ZIP file
4. Verify you have everything needed
