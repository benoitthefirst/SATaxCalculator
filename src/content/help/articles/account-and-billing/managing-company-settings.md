# Managing Company Settings

Configure your company details and business preferences in ProcessX.

## Overview

Company settings contain information about your business that ProcessX uses for:
- Tax calculations
- Report generation
- Invoice details
- SARS compliance

## Accessing Company Settings

1. Go to **Settings** from the dashboard
2. Click **Company** or **Company Settings**
3. Or click your company name in the navigation

## Business Information

### Basic Details

| Field | Description | Example |
|-------|-------------|---------|
| Company Name | Registered business name | ABC Consulting (Pty) Ltd |
| Trading Name | Name you trade as | ABC Consulting |
| Registration Number | Company registration | 2020/123456/07 |
| Business Type | Legal structure | (Pty) Ltd |

### Contact Information

| Field | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| Physical Address | Business premises address |
| Postal Address | Mailing address |
| Phone Number | Business contact |
| Email | Business email |
| Website | Company website |

## Tax Information

### Income Tax

| Field | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| Income Tax Number | SARS tax reference |
| Tax Year End | Your financial year end |
| Taxpayer Type | Individual, Company, Trust |

### VAT Registration

| Field | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| VAT Registered | Yes/No toggle |
| VAT Number | SARS VAT registration |
| VAT Period | Monthly or Bi-monthly |

## Industry and Category

Select your business sector:
- Professional Services
- Retail
- Manufacturing
- Technology
- Construction
- Healthcare
- Other

This helps with:
- Relevant expense categories
- Industry benchmarks
- Appropriate reports

## Currency Settings

| Setting | Options |
|---------|---------|
| Primary Currency | ZAR (South African Rand) |
| Currency Display | R 1,000.00 or ZAR 1,000.00 |
| Decimal Places | 2 (standard) |

## Updating Company Details

### Step 1: Navigate to Settings

Go to **Settings** > **Company**.

### Step 2: Edit Information

Click **Edit** or directly modify fields.

### Step 3: Save Changes

Click **Save** to update.

Changes take effect immediately.

## Company Logo

Add your company logo:

1. Click **Upload Logo**
2. Select image file
3. Crop if needed
4. Save

Logo appears on:
- Dashboard
- Exported reports
- Team invitations

Requirements:
- PNG or JPG format
- Maximum 2MB
- Square or rectangular

## Multi-Company Setup

With Business or Enterprise plans:

### Adding a Company

1. Click **Add Company** in company switcher
2. Fill in company details
3. Configure settings
4. Save

### Switching Companies

1. Click company name in navigation
2. Select another company
3. View switches to that company

### Company-Specific Settings

Each company has independent:
- Tax settings
- Categories
- Team members
- Data and reports

## Team and Permissions

Manage who can access and edit:

1. Go to **Settings** > **Team**
2. See current team members
3. Invite new members
4. Set roles and permissions

Roles include:
- **Owner**: Full access, billing
- **Admin**: Full access, no billing
- **Member**: Limited access
- **Viewer**: Read-only access
- **Accountant**: Reports and exports only

## Deleting a Company

To remove a company:

1. Switch to the company
2. Go to **Settings** > **Company**
3. Scroll to **Danger Zone**
4. Click **Delete Company**
5. Confirm deletion

Warning: This deletes all company data permanently.
