# Upgrading Your Subscription

How to upgrade or change your ProcessX subscription plan.

## Overview

As your business grows, you may need more features. Upgrading your ProcessX subscription is quick and your new features are available immediately.

## When to Upgrade

Consider upgrading if you:
- Hit usage limits (receipts, AI analyses)
- Need team collaboration
- Require advanced reports
- Want vehicle logbook features
- Need multiple companies
- Require API access

## How to Upgrade

### Step 1: Go to Subscription Settings

1. Click **Settings** in the navigation
2. Select **Subscription**
3. Or click **Upgrade** when prompted

### Step 2: Choose a Plan

Review available plans:
- Compare features
- Check pricing
- Consider annual discount

### Step 3: Select Billing Cycle

Choose:
- **Monthly**: Pay month-to-month, cancel anytime
- **Annual**: Pay upfront, save 17% (2 months free)

### Step 4: Enter Payment Details

Provide payment information:
- Credit/Debit card
- Or other available methods

### Step 5: Confirm and Upgrade

1. Review your selection
2. Accept terms
3. Click **Upgrade Now**

Your new plan activates immediately.

## What Happens When You Upgrade

### Immediate Access

All new features are available right away:
- No waiting period
- No restart required
- Just refresh your browser

### Billing

- **Pro-rated**: You only pay the difference for the current period
- **Future billing**: Next invoice reflects new plan price
- **Receipt**: Confirmation email sent

### Data

- All existing data is preserved
- No migration needed
- History fully accessible

### Starter to Professional

### Professional to Business

Gain access to:

## Downgrading

If you need fewer features:

1. Go to **Settings** > **Subscription**
2. Click **Change Plan**
3. Select a lower tier
4. Confirm downgrade

### Downgrade Considerations

- Takes effect at next billing cycle
- Features above new plan limit become restricted
- Data is preserved but may be read-only
- Team members may lose access

## Cancelling Your Subscription

To cancel:

1. Go to **Settings** > **Subscription**
2. Click **Cancel Subscription**
3. Select cancellation reason
4. Confirm cancellation

### After Cancellation

- Access continues until period ends
- Data preserved for 30 days
- Can reactivate anytime
- Exports available

## Billing Issues

### Failed Payment

If payment fails:
- You'll receive email notification
- 3-day grace period provided
- Update payment method to continue
- Account may be restricted after grace period

### Updating Payment Method

1. Go to **Settings** > **Subscription**
2. Click **Payment Methods**
3. Add new card
4. Remove old card if needed

### Invoices and Receipts

Access billing history:
1. Go to **Settings** > **Subscription**
2. Click **Billing History**
3. Download invoices as PDF
