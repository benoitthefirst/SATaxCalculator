# Understanding Your Plan

Learn about ProcessX subscription tiers and the features included in each plan.

## Overview

ProcessX offers subscription plans to fit different business needs, from free for solo freelancers to Enterprise for large organizations.

## Available Plans

| Mobile Access | Yes |

| Team Members | 1 (you) |

## Viewing Your Current Plan

1. Go to **Settings** > **Subscription**
2. See your current plan details
3. View usage and limits
4. See billing information

## Plan Features Explained

### Receipt Uploads

Number of receipt images/PDFs you can upload per month.

### AI Document Analysis

Uses AI to extract expense details from receipts automatically.

### Vehicle Logbook

Track business travel and calculate SARS deductions.

### Multiple Companies

Manage separate businesses from one account.

### Team Members

Invite others to collaborate on your account.

### API Access

Connect ProcessX to other software programmatically.

## Usage Tracking

Monitor your usage:

1. Go to **Settings** > **Subscription**
2. View **Usage** section
3. See:
   - Receipts uploaded this month
   - AI analyses used
   - Storage used
   - Team members invited

## Plan Limits

When you reach a limit:
- You'll see a warning notification
- Some features may be restricted
- Upgrade options are presented
- Data is never deleted

## Choosing the Right Plan

| If You Are... | Recommended Plan |
|---------------|------------------|
| Just starting freelancing | Starter (Free) |
| Growing freelancer | Essential |
| Established freelancer | Professional |
| Small business with team | Business |
| Large organization | Enterprise |

## Annual vs Monthly Billing

Save with annual billing:
- Monthly: Standard price
- Annual: 2 months free (17% savings)

Switch billing cycle at **Settings** > **Subscription**.
