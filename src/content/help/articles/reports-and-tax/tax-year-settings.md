# Tax Year Settings

Configure your tax year and understand South African tax periods.

## Overview

Setting up your tax year correctly in ProcessX ensures:
- Accurate tax calculations
- Correct report periods
- Proper provisional tax planning
- SARS-aligned record keeping

## South African Tax Year

### Individual Tax Year

For individuals, sole proprietors, and most small businesses, [SARS](https://www.sars.gov.za/) uses the following tax year:

| Tax Year | Start Date | End Date |
|----------|------------|----------|
| 2023/2024 | 1 March 2023 | 29 February 2024 |
| 2024/2025 | 1 March 2024 | 28 February 2025 |
| 2025/2026 | 1 March 2025 | 28 February 2026 |

This is the **default** in ProcessX. Learn more about [tax years on SARS](https://www.sars.gov.za/types-of-tax/personal-income-tax/tax-season/).

### Company Tax Year

Companies can have different year-ends:
- Financial year set at registration with [CIPC](https://www.cipc.co.za/)
- Often aligned with calendar year (December)
- Or June year-end
- Or any month

If your company has a non-standard year-end, configure this in settings.

## Configuring Tax Year

### For Individuals (Default)

ProcessX defaults to the March-February tax year. No configuration needed.

### For Companies

1. Go to **Settings** > **Company**
2. Find **Financial Year End**
3. Select your year-end month
4. Save changes

Reports and calculations will align to your year-end.

Calculations use the [SARS tax tables](https://www.sars.gov.za/tax-rates/income-tax/) for the relevant year.

## Tax Deadlines

### Income Tax Return (ITR12)

SARS announces specific [filing season dates](https://www.sars.gov.za/types-of-tax/personal-income-tax/tax-season/) each year:

| Filer Type | Typical Deadline |
|------------|----------|
| Non-provisional (manual) | Usually November |
| Non-provisional (eFiling) | December |
| Provisional taxpayers | End of January |

File your return on [SARS eFiling](https://www.sarsefiling.co.za/).

### Provisional Tax (IRP6)

For [provisional taxpayers](https://www.sars.gov.za/types-of-tax/provisional-tax/):

| Payment | Due Date |
|---------|----------|
| First provisional | End of August |
| Second provisional | End of February |
| Third (top-up) | 7 months after year-end |

### VAT Returns

Depending on your [VAT registration category](https://www.sars.gov.za/types-of-tax/value-added-tax/):
- Monthly: Last business day of following month
- Bi-monthly: Last business day of following month

## Changing Tax Year

If your company changes its financial year-end:

1. Consult your accountant first
2. Notify SARS of the change via [eFiling](https://www.sarsefiling.co.za/)
3. Update ProcessX settings
4. Note: May result in a short tax year

## Historical Data

ProcessX maintains your historical data:
- View any past tax year

- Export historical records
- Compare year-over-year

## Multiple Companies

If managing multiple companies with different year-ends:
- Each company has its own settings
- Switch companies to see correct periods
- Generate reports per company
- Consolidation available (Business plan)

## Tax Year vs Calendar Year

| Period | Use For |
|--------|---------|
| Tax Year | SARS returns, tax calculations |
| Calendar Year | Some VAT periods, contracts |
| Financial Year | Annual reports, budgets |

ProcessX supports all, with tax year as default.
