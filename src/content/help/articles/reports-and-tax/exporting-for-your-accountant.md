# Exporting for Your Accountant

Share your financial data with your accountant or tax practitioner efficiently.

## Overview

When working with an accountant, they'll need access to your financial records. ProcessX makes this easy with comprehensive export options designed for professional review.

## What Your Accountant Needs

Typically, accountants require:

### Essential Documents
- Income summary
- Expense summary with categories
- Profit & Loss statement
- Receipt copies or access

### Tax-Specific Documents
- Deduction summary
- Travel logbook (if applicable)
- VAT summary (if VAT registered)
- Tax computation estimate

### Supporting Documents
- Bank statements (from your bank)
- Invoices issued
- Major contracts

## How to Export

### Option 1: Individual Reports

Export each report separately:

1. Go to **Reports**
2. Select the report type
3. Choose date range (usually full tax year)
4. Click **Export** and select PDF or Excel

### Option 2: Comprehensive Package

Export all reports at once:

1. Go to **Reports** > **Export All**
2. Select the tax year
3. Choose what to include
4. Click **Download Package**

You'll receive a ZIP file with all reports.

### Option 3: Grant Access

Invite your accountant to view your ProcessX account:

1. Go to **Settings** > **Team**
2. Click **Invite Member**
3. Enter accountant's email
4. Select **Accountant** role (read-only)
5. They receive access to view your data

## Export Formats

| Format | Best For |
|--------|----------|
| PDF | Final reports, easy viewing |
| Excel | Analysis, verification |
| CSV | Importing to accounting software |

## What to Include

### For Annual Tax Return

| Document | Purpose |
|----------|---------|
| Income Summary | Total revenue for ITR12 |
| Deduction Summary | Total deductions for ITR12 |
| Tax Computation | Estimated tax calculation |
| P&L Statement | Overall financial position |
| Travel Logbook | Vehicle deduction support |

### For Provisional Tax

| Document | Purpose |
|----------|---------|
| Year-to-date Income | Estimate annual income |
| Year-to-date Expenses | Estimate annual deductions |
| Tax Estimate | Provisional payment amount |

### For VAT Returns

| Document | Purpose |
|----------|---------|
| VAT Summary | Output and input VAT |
| Income with VAT | VAT on sales |
| Expenses with VAT | Claimable input VAT |

## Preparing Clean Data

Before exporting, verify:

1. **All income recorded**: Check against bank deposits
2. **All expenses captured**: Review bank statements
3. **Correct categories**: Expenses properly classified
4. **Receipts attached**: Documentation complete
5. **Period correct**: Right date range selected

## Working with Your Accountant

### Initial Handover

When first engaging an accountant:
- Export full history available
- Explain your business operations
- Share access credentials if granting access
- Schedule kickoff meeting

### Ongoing Monthly

- Export monthly summaries
- Flag any unusual transactions
- Note questions for them
- Keep communication regular

### Year-End

- Export complete tax year data
- Include all supporting documents
- Answer their questions promptly
- Review before submission

## Security Considerations

When sharing data:
- Use secure file transfer
- Don't email sensitive data unprotected
- Use ProcessX's accountant access feature
- Verify accountant's identity
- Keep access updated

## Common Questions

### What if my accountant uses different software?

Excel and CSV exports work with most accounting software. Your accountant can import the data.

### Do I need to print receipts?

No. Digital copies exported from ProcessX are SARS compliant. Your accountant can review them digitally.

### How far back can I export?

ProcessX retains your data for as long as your account is active. You can export any historical period.
