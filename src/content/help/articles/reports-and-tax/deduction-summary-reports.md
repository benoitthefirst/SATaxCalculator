# Deduction Summary Reports

View all your tax-deductible expenses in one comprehensive report.

## Overview

The Deduction Summary Report consolidates all your deductible expenses, organized by category, making it easy to:
- See total deductions at a glance
- Complete your tax return (ITR12)
- Share with your accountant
- Verify you've claimed everything

## Accessing the Deduction Summary

1. Go to **Reports** from the navigation
2. Select **Deduction Summary**
3. Choose the tax year
4. View or export the report

## What's Included

The report shows all expenses that qualify as tax deductions:

### Operating Expenses
- Office supplies and consumables
- Telephone and internet
- Utilities (business portion)
- Rent (business premises)
- Insurance

### Professional Services
- Accounting and bookkeeping fees
- Legal fees (non-capital)
- Consulting fees
- Professional subscriptions

### Travel and Entertainment
- Business travel
- Accommodation
- Meals (50% deductible for entertainment)
- Subsistence allowances

### Marketing and Advertising
- Advertising costs
- Marketing materials
- Website expenses

### Vehicle Expenses
- Fuel (business proportion)
- Maintenance (business proportion)
- Insurance (business proportion)
- Or: Travel allowance calculation

### Home Office
- Proportional rent/bond interest
- Proportional utilities
- Home office equipment

## Report Structure

| Category | Amount |

### Detailed Section

Each category broken down by:
- Individual expenses
- Dates
- Amounts
- Supporting notes

## Using for ITR12

When completing your SARS tax return, the deduction summary maps to:

| Report Category | ITR12 Section |
|-----------------|---------------|
| Travel expenses | Code 4020 |
| Vehicle allowance | Code 3702 |
| Home office | Code 4028 |
| Other expenses | Code 4020 |

Consult the ITR12 guide or your accountant for exact placement.

## What's NOT Included

Some expenses are not deductible and are excluded:

- Personal expenses
- Entertainment (non-business)
- Fines and penalties
- Capital expenditure (handled as depreciation)
- Donations (separate section on ITR12)

If you've miscategorized an expense, it won't appear here.

## Verifying Deductions

Use this report to check:

### Completeness
- Have you recorded all expenses?
- Are any months missing entries?
- Did you forget regular expenses?

### Accuracy
- Are amounts correct?
- Are categories appropriate?
- Is there documentation for each?

### Eligibility
- Is each expense business-related?
- Does it meet SARS requirements?
- Would it survive an audit?

## Comparing Tax Years

Compare deductions across years:

1. Generate report for current year
2. Generate report for previous year
3. Compare totals and categories
4. Investigate significant differences

## Exporting

Export the deduction summary as:
- **PDF**: For records and sharing
- **Excel**: For further analysis
- **CSV**: For other software

Click **Export** and select format.

## Best Practices

1. **Generate monthly**: Catch missing expenses early
2. **Review categories**: Ensure correct classification
3. **Attach receipts**: Support every deduction
4. **Reconcile**: Match to bank statements
5. **Consult professionals**: For complex situations
